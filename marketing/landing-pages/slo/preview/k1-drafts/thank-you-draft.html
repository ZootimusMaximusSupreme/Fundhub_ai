Common questions
Is Fundhub a lender?
No. Fundhub is a funding advisory. We qualify you across a nationwide network of funding partners and manage the process end to end, so you get matched to lenders that fit your file instead of guessing at banks one by one.
What makes Fundhub different?
The engine. UnderwriteIQ reads your credit the way a bank underwriter actually reads it, not the way Credit Karma shows it to you, then matches your file against thousands of lenders and finds the 30 to 50 that actually fit. We call that the Conveyor Belt. From there we submit in rounds, keeping your credit clean between each one, so we can go after real money without wrecking your score on the way. Your file runs with a Pod: a funding advisor, a credit specialist, and an ops specialist, not one account manager juggling fifty files.
Will this affect my credit score?
No. The pull we run on your call is a tri-bureau soft inquiry. Zero score impact. Hard inquiries only ever happen later, with your consent, when an actual funding application goes out.
Do I need a business to qualify?
No. We fund individuals too. If you have a business, we can qualify it alongside your personal profile and route you to whichever gets you the most capital.
What if my credit isn't great?
Still take the call. If the numbers aren't fundable yet, we tell you straight and you leave with the exact fix. We handle repair too, so there's a route either way.
What does this cost?
The diagnostic soft pull on your call is $32, and it goes straight to the credit bureau cost. Beyond that we're performance-based and it's all in writing: if we don't act on your file within 24 business hours, you don't pay. If applications don't go out within 72 business hours, you don't pay. If you qualify and we don't secure funding, you don't pay. Exact numbers get walked through live on your call.
Do I have to go done-for-you?
No. There are two ways to run this. Done-for-you, where your advisor and the system run your funding rounds for you. Or do-it-yourself, our education route that hands you the same playbook for funding and credit repair to run on your own. You pick on the call.
Can I make money referring people to Fundhub?
Yes. If you know business owners or anyone who needs capital, our affiliate program pays you for every referral that funds. Details and sign-up at fundhub.ai/affiliates.
How fast does funding move?
Once you're qualified and moving, best case you can see funds in a matter of days. Most clients are funded within a week to ten days. Your advisor maps your exact timeline on the call.
