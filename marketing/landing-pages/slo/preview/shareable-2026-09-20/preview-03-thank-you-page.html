Common questions
Is Fundhub a lender?
No. Fundhub is a funding advisory. We qualify you across a nationwide network of funding partners and manage the process end to end, so you get matched to lenders that fit your file instead of guessing at banks one by one.
What makes Fundhub different?
The engine. UnderwriteIQ reads your credit the way a bank underwriter reads it, then matches your file against thousands of lenders and finds the 30 to 50 that actually fit. We call that the Conveyor Belt. From there we submit in rounds, keeping your credit clean between each one, so we can go after real money while protecting your score. Your file runs with a Pod: a funding advisor, a credit specialist, and an ops specialist.
Will this affect my credit score?
No. The pull we ran when you bought your roadmap was a tri-bureau soft inquiry. Zero score impact. Hard inquiries only ever happen later, with your consent, when an actual funding application goes out.
Do I need a business to qualify?
No. We fund individuals too. If you have a business, we can qualify it alongside your personal profile and route you to whichever gets you the most capital.
What if my credit isn't great?
Still take the call. Your roadmap already says what needs fixing and in what order. On the call we go through it with you, and if you'd rather we run the credit optimization for you, that's a route too.
What does this cost?
Your roadmap is paid for and it's yours either way. The call is free. If it makes sense for us to run more of it for you, we're performance-based and it's all in writing: if we don't act on your file within 24 business hours, you don't pay. If applications don't go out within 72 business hours, you don't pay. If you qualify and we don't secure funding, you don't pay. Exact numbers get walked through live on your call.
Do I have to go done-for-you?
No. You can run the roadmap yourself, on your own timeline, and it works. Or we run it with you. You pick on the call.
Can I make money referring people to Fundhub?
Yes. If you know business owners or anyone who needs capital, our affiliate program pays you 20% of the sale price on every person you send who buys, and 5% on anyone they send. Details and sign-up at fundhub.ai/affiliates.
How fast does funding move?
Once you're qualified and moving, best case you can see funds in a matter of days. Most clients are funded within a week to ten days. Your advisor maps your exact timeline on the call.
