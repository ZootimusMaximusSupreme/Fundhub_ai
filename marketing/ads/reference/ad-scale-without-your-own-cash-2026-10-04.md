## How it was built (repeat this)

1. Pulled every point Chris made about this ad across all of his voice messages in the thread, in
   his order: the Jeremy Haynes point, the $10K / $20K / $50K a month range and people starting a
   business, freedom and time with family, "I've been there", the family wanting you to take the
   leap, the risk quote, "you might not be ready, start getting ready now", the economy getting
   harder and rates rising, the three steps, driving bank to bank, do it yourself or with someone.
2. Kept his own phrases and stories ("If it's not with me, do it with somebody", "I want you to win",
   "I've been there", driving to every bank in his state).
3. Did not cut points for length. Earlier drafts that trimmed his points got rejected.
4. Turned his spoken ramble into full, plain sentences at a 5th grade level, keeping his rhythm.
   No "dude" or "bro" filler, no hype.
5. Then applied the rules: dollar amounts as numerals, delivery marks, open loop on line 2 paid off
   before the CTA, no telling the viewer what they are, nothing that makes them feel stupid.
6. Left out on purpose: "you're cooked" and "live like a poor person" (make the viewer feel stupid).
   Jeremy Haynes is paraphrased; swap in his exact words if the video turns up.

## Script

Putting MORE money into your business is the fastest way to stop running out of cash.

And you can do it without using your own cash. ↑

Here's what I found scaling my own businesses. Jeremy Haynes, one of the biggest names in ads, explains this all the time. When your business is stuck with limited cash, you have to turn your spending UP. More spend gets you more data, your ads perform better, and you can finally pay for the people and tools that help you break free.

Maybe your business makes $10,000 a month, maybe $20,000 a month, maybe $50,000 a month. Or maybe you don't have a business yet and you're looking to start one. Wherever you're starting, if you want to get past $100,000 a month, you need capital to get there.

I'm in my early 30s, and I know how hard it is to build real freedom on limited capital. It's hard to save for retirement, and it's even harder to buy back the time with your family that you missed the last few years while you were grinding on your business.

Everybody knows it. I know it, because I've been there. Sometimes you're not taking the right action, and sometimes you just don't have enough capital to take the right action.

You already know what your business needs. Maybe you don't have the money to do it. Or maybe you have the money, but it's your savings, and you don't want to risk it. ↑

Your family wants you to take that leap. Your mom, your brother, your sister, your son, your daughter. They all want to see you win.

Taking that leap with your own savings is scary, and you don't have to.

Mark Zuckerberg said, "The biggest risk is not taking any risk."

You might not be ready to take that risk yet, and that's okay. But start getting ready NOW. The economy is getting harder for entrepreneurs. Interest rates just went up, banks are giving out less money, and the business owners who get ready FIRST are the ones who still get funded.

Here's the plan, and one step in it took me years of driving bank to bank to figure out. ↑

Step one, fix up your credit FIRST. If it's not with me, do it with somebody. I want you to win either way. Just get it right.

Step two, open your businesses and build their credit. If you have extra cash, buy an older company. Those are assets banks will fund. Your personal credit is the main one, and every business you add MULTIPLIES it.

Step three is the one that took me years. You need to know exactly which banks fit your credit profile and your business profile, and apply to them in the right order. ↑

I used to drive to every single bank in my state to learn what each one looks for. You'll learn a lot doing that, and it's worth doing. But it takes a TON of time when you also have a business to run. That's time away from your business and your family, and it makes everything harder.

Working with someone who already knows every bank and every detail they look for saves you all of that time.

Once you have that plan, you have everything you need to get funded. If you feel confident doing it yourself, go do it. Or work with someone who's done this their whole career. That's the plan while interest rates rise.

And that's how you scale without touching your own cash. We get you funding on your credit and your businesses, so you put that money into growth, your cash stays right where it is, and you finally get the freedom and the time back that you've been working for.

Click the link below, and your advisor will walk you through the plan for where your credit is right now and show you how much funding you can get to grow your business.
