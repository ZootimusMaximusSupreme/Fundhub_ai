## Start here

The condition of your credit determines where you are in the funding process. Wherever you are, Fundhub has a solution for that phase.

Chris, Fundhub's founder, has spent a decade in this industry, funded a little over $1,000,000 for himself, and pulled $100,000 credit lines on four different companies off one personal credit file. Every file Fundhub works runs through UnderwriteIQ, our own underwriting software, built from hundreds of files and thousands of data points.

Each offer below shows what you get and why it's worth it. The table at the end shows where to start.

## Credit optimization starter: $200

For $200, Fundhub runs the first two rounds of credit optimization on your file. You see the results on your own file before you decide on the full program.

### What it solves

If you have paid for help before and never saw what was done, the starter shows you every result in your client portal. If you worry that starting will make your file worse, you start small on your own file and see each result before you go further.

### What you get

- Fundhub runs two dispute rounds on your file for you.
- Each letter is a Metro 2 letter, sent with Fundhub's own dispute and mailing strategy so items come off faster.
- You track your progress in your client portal.

### Why it's worth it

The starter lets you watch the process work on your own file for $200. Every dispute and every bureau response shows up in your portal. The $200 counts toward the full program, so moving up to all six rounds costs $800 more.

## Full credit optimization: $1,000

For $1,000, Fundhub works your file for up to six rounds to get it ready for funding.

### What it solves

Lenders read your inquiries, old addresses and negative items along with your score, so a good score can still end in a denial. The six rounds clean those items up. Each round works on the items still reporting on your file until the file is ready for funding.

### What you get

- Fundhub runs up to six dispute rounds on your file for you.
- Letters go to the credit bureaus, and straight to the creditor that reported an item when that is what it takes.
- Negative items, inquiries and old addresses get cleaned up on your file.
- You track your progress in your portal, and you get updates the whole way.
- You can finance the program if your credit score is 600 or higher.

### Why it's worth it

The condition of your file determines how much funding you get approved for, so cleaning it up raises what you qualify for. Each month your file stays the same, you get approved for less than an optimized file gets. Your letters are built on the FCRA and Metro 2, and an item that stays on your file gets escalated to the CFPB and your state attorney general. When your file is ready, you move straight into funding with the same team.

## Capital Blueprint: $5,000 to $10,000

Capital Blueprint runs your credit through UnderwriteIQ and turns it into a step-by-step plan to get you funded as fast as possible. A team and a system keep you on that plan until your file is ready.

### What it solves

After a denial with no clear reason, UnderwriteIQ reviews your file the way a lender does, so you see what the bank sees before your next application. Every step in your roadmap comes from your own file. Your Bank & Lender Match List shows which banks approve files like yours and the order to apply in. The agent and your CSM keep you on each step until it is done.

### What you get

- You get a Credit Analysis Report built from your real credit file.
- You get a Credit Optimization Roadmap that lists every step in order.
- You get a Dispute Letter Pack that tells you how and when to send each letter.
- You get a Funding Snapshot that shows how much we think you'll qualify for based on where you're at right now.
- You get a Bank & Lender Match List of the banks that approve files like yours.
- Fundhub runs a soft pull every 30 days and updates your letters to match the changes on your file.
- An accountability agent texts you, emails you and pings your portal. It breaks each task into small steps, answers your questions and keeps each step open until you finish it.
- Your own CSM checks in with you and makes sure the work gets done.
- Finance OS is coming soon. It includes a paydown simulator, payment timing and tracking for your promo periods after you're funded. If you buy now, your first 12 months are free.
- When your file is ready, an alert goes to your closer, who reaches out to run your funding.
- You can add a spouse, partner or family member as a second applicant.
- You can finance Capital Blueprint.

### Why it's worth it

Fundhub builds your plan by running your credit through UnderwriteIQ, its own underwriting software. Your Funding Snapshot shows what you qualify for today, what you qualify for after optimization, and the money left on the table between the two. The agent and your CSM keep every step moving, so the plan gets finished. The soft pull every 30 days keeps your letters matched to what changed on your file. You save the time and money it takes to learn this on your own, and when your file is ready, your closer runs your funding.

## Funding, done for you: $1,000 to $3,000 down

Fundhub runs your funding from start to finish. The upfront fee is taken only once you're confirmed qualified for funding.

### What it solves

Every denial adds a hard inquiry to your report, so applying to one lender after another piles up denials. Fundhub applies only to the banks that approve files like yours, in planned rounds. Hundreds of files and thousands of data points show which banks approve which files.

### What you get

- Fundhub optimizes your file for the maximum amount of funding.
- Fundhub opens your businesses if you need them.
- Fundhub picks the banks that approve files like yours, using thousands of data points.
- Your applications go out in the right order, across 3 to 6 rounds in your funding sequence.
- A specialist removes inquiries between rounds.
- You can get business funding, personal funding, or both.
- You track every round in your own portal, and you get updates the whole way.
- You put $1,000 to $3,000 down, plus a success fee on the funding you get, which is set on your call. You can finance the deposit if you have verifiable income and high credit.

### Why it's worth it

The banks you pick and the order you apply in determine how much you get approved for, and Fundhub runs that part for you. Your fee is taken only once you're confirmed qualified for funding, and your deposit counts toward your success fee. Every round goes in with the inquiries from the last round removed. The average Fundhub client gets $100,000 to $300,000 in funding.

## Questions people ask

**Is Fundhub legit?**
Chris has spent a decade in this industry and worked hundreds of files. On your call, you see how the process works on your own file before you commit to anything.

**How do I know you won't take my money and disappear?**
Your closer walks you through the agreement on screen before you get a payment link. After you pay, you get your client portal and the next person on your team, like your funding advisor.

**I applied before and got denied. Why would this go differently?**
Your file at the time and the banks you picked determined that denial. UnderwriteIQ reads your file as it is today and shows which banks approve files like yours, and the order to apply in.

**Will this put a lot of inquiries on my report?**
Your first look is a soft pull, which leaves your score where it is. Applications go in planned rounds, and a specialist removes inquiries between rounds.

**Do I need a business?**
You do not need a business. You can get funded on your personal file, your business, or both, and we open businesses for you if you need them.

**Can I finance it?**
Capital Blueprint can be financed. Credit optimization can be financed with a credit score of 600 or higher. The funding deposit can be financed when you have verifiable income and high credit.

**What if I don't get funded?**
Your funding fee is taken only once you're confirmed qualified for funding. Before that, UnderwriteIQ shows how much we think you'll qualify for based on where you're at right now.

**Can my spouse or partner join?**
Yes, you can bring them on your call. With Capital Blueprint, you can also add them as a second applicant.

**Can you prove it works before I pay for the bigger offers?**
Your proof step is the $32 soft pull on your call or the $200 starter. You see the process work on your own file first.

**Other companies charge $8,000 to $10,000. What's the catch?**
Every Fundhub price is in this document. Funding is $1,000 to $3,000 down, and that deposit counts toward your success fee.

**I already have cards. Why would I need this?**
Your call finds what you want next: more room, better terms, or a cleaner file.

**I don't want more debt.**
Funding puts real credit in your name, and you decide how much of it to use. Credit optimization and Capital Blueprint get your file ready first, with no new accounts.

**Do I have to personally guarantee the credit?**
Each bank sets its own rules on personal guarantees. Ask about them on your call.

**I need to think about it.**
Tell your closer the one piece that's still unclear: your file, the fee, or trust. Each one has a straight answer.
