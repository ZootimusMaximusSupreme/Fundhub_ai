## Start here

The condition of your credit determines where you are in the funding process. Wherever you are, Fundhub has a solution for that phase.

Chris, Fundhub's founder, has spent a decade in this industry, funded a little over $1,000,000 for himself, and pulled $100,000 credit lines on four different companies off one personal credit file. Every file Fundhub works runs through UnderwriteIQ, our own underwriting software, built from hundreds of files and thousands of data points.

Each offer below shows what you get and why it's worth it. The table at the end shows where to start.

## Credit optimization starter: $200

For $200, Fundhub runs your first two rounds of credit optimization, so you see results on your own file before you commit to the full program.

**What it solves**

- **Paying for help and seeing nothing.** Your first two rounds cost $200, and every result shows in your portal.
- **Worrying that starting makes things worse.** You start small, on your own file, and see each result before you go further.

**What you get**

- Two dispute rounds on your file, done for you
- Metro 2 letters sent with Fundhub's own dispute and mailing strategy, built to get items off faster
- Your progress tracked in your client portal

**Why buy it**

- You see results on your own file for $200 before you commit to anything bigger.
- It's the proof step: you watch the process work on your file.
- Every dispute and every bureau response shows up in your portal.
- The $200 counts toward the full program. Once you see the results, moving up to all six rounds costs $800 more.

## Full credit optimization: $1,000

For $1,000, Fundhub works your file for up to six rounds to get it ready for funding.

**What it solves**

- **A good score and still a denial.** Lenders read your inquiries, old addresses and negative items too, so six rounds clean them up.
- **A file that never changes.** Each round works the items still reporting until your file is ready for funding.

**What you get**

- Up to six dispute rounds, done for you
- Letters to the bureaus, and straight to the creditor that reported an item when that's what it takes
- Negative items, inquiries and old addresses cleaned up on your file
- Your progress tracked in your portal, with updates the whole way
- Financing available with a credit score of 600 or higher

**Why buy it**

- The condition of your file determines how much funding you get approved for, so cleaning it up is the step that raises what you qualify for.
- Every month your file stays as it is, you get approved for less than an optimized file gets.
- Your letters are built on the FCRA and Metro 2, and an item that stays on your file gets escalated to the CFPB and your state attorney general.
- The work is done for you, and when your file is ready, you move straight into funding with the same team.

- **Starting a plan and stalling on your own.** The agent and your CSM keep you on each step until it's done.

**What you get**

- Finance OS, coming soon: a paydown simulator, payment timing, and tracking of your promo periods after you're funded. Buy now and your first 12 months are free
- A ready-for-funding alert: when your file is ready, your closer reaches out to run your funding
- The option to add a spouse, partner or family member as a second applicant
- Financing available

**Why buy it**

- Fundhub is the only company in the industry that runs your credit through an underwriting algorithm to build your plan.

- The soft pull every 30 days keeps your letters matched to what changed on your file.

## Questions people ask

**Is Fundhub legit?**
Chris has spent a decade in this industry and worked hundreds of files. On your call, you see how the process works on your own file before you commit to anything.

**How do I know you won't take my money and disappear?**
Your closer walks you through the agreement on screen before you get a payment link. After you pay, you get your client portal and the next person on your team, like your funding advisor.

**I applied before and got denied. Why would this go differently?**
Your file at the time and the banks you picked determined that denial. UnderwriteIQ reads your file as it is today and shows which banks approve files like yours, and the order to apply in.

**Will this put a lot of inquiries on my report?**
Your first look is a soft pull, which leaves your score where it is. Applications go in planned rounds, and a specialist removes inquiries between rounds.

**Do I need a business?**
No. You get funded on your personal file, your business, or both, and we open businesses for you if you need them.

**Can I finance it?**
Capital Blueprint can be financed. Credit optimization can be financed with a credit score of 600 or higher. The funding deposit can be financed when you have verifiable income and high credit.

**What if I don't get funded?**
Your funding fee is taken only once you're confirmed qualified for funding. Before that, UnderwriteIQ shows how much we think you'll qualify for based on where you're at right now.

**Can my spouse or partner join?**
Yes. Bring them on your call. With Capital Blueprint, you can also add them as a second applicant.

**Can you prove it works before I pay for the bigger offers?**
Your proof step is the $32 soft pull on your call or the $200 starter. You see the process work on your own file first.

**Other companies charge $8,000 to $10,000. What's the catch?**
Every Fundhub price is in this document. Funding is $1,000 to $3,000 down, and that deposit counts toward your success fee.

**I already have cards. Why would I need this?**
Your call finds what you want next: more room, better terms, or a cleaner file.

**I don't want more debt.**
Funding puts real credit in your name, and you decide how much of it to use. Credit optimization and Capital Blueprint get your file ready first, with no new accounts.

**Do I have to personally guarantee the credit?**
Each bank sets its own rules on personal guarantees. Ask about them on your call.

**I need to think about it.**
Tell your closer the one piece that's still unclear: your file, the fee, or trust. Each one has a straight answer.
