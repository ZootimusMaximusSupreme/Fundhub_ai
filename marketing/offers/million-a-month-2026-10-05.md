# Offers that can reach $1M a month

Owner ask, 2026-10-05: "find some offers that I can scale to $1 million a month," across Fundhub, Yesdoor and the 60 Minute Offer.

Ranked by how fast each one gets there. Every figure comes from Chris, the repo, or the arithmetic shown. None of them is guessed.

## 1. Fundhub done-for-you funding (already selling)

- Average order value per funded client is $10,000 to $15,000.
- $1,000,000 ÷ $12,500 = **80 funded clients a month**, about 4 every working day.
- Ads are the cheap part. Booked calls already cost $30 to $35.
- The limit is the team. With four closers, each one closes 20 funded clients a month. One funding advisor has to fulfill all 80 files.

## 2. White label (multiplies offer 1)

- Terms: $10,000 upfront, 50% of funding and credit optimization, and a floor of 10 clients a month per partner.
- One partner at the floor, with those clients funding at $12,500: 10 × $12,500 × 50% = **$62,500 a month to Fundhub**.
- **16 partners at the floor = $1,000,000 a month**, plus $160,000 in upfront fees.
- Fundhub already does the fulfillment, so this runs on the same desk as offer 1.
- Blocked on two numbers Chris still has to set: the build clock and the first-file guarantee.
- The buyer pool is small on purpose. Buyers need cash or funding and must already run a company.

## 3. Capital Blueprint at $5,000 to $10,000 (financed on ClarityPay)

- $1,000,000 ÷ $7,500 = **133 sales a month**.
- The buyer is the 550 to 700 file that funding can't take today, which is most of the leads.
- It earns on leads offer 1 turns away. When the file is ready, the closer sells funding to the same person a second time.

## 4. Alternative finance (SBA, hard money, real estate)

- Launching within about 30 days. Chris believes almost no one runs ads in this space.
- Fewer deals with bigger checks. The fee per deal isn't set yet, so there's no math here until it is.

## 5. The 60 Minute Offer

- As written in `60min/README.md`, it is a private system for Chris with no customers.
- It also has no price (`[PRICE]` in all three tiers), and the domain isn't bought.
- The hour isn't proven yet. Merchant accounts, ad accounts and phone numbers still need a person's identity check.
- To sell it: through FH Consulting at up to $20,000, **50 launches a month = $1,000,000**. Every launch is custom delivery, so it hits the same team limit an agency does.
- Best use today: the engine that launches Fundhub's next offers in an hour. The Replica skill pack makes it start from a competitor's proven offer.

## 6. Yesdoor

- Renters pay nothing. Buildings pay a share of the first month's rent, on signed agreements.
- Taking a placement fee likely needs a real estate licence under a broker. Check this first (see `yesdoor/README.md`).
- $1,000,000 a month means hundreds of signed leases a month in one metro. This is the slowest of the six.
- Best use: every renter the soft pull says no to is a Fundhub credit optimization lead.

Put the next 90 days into offers 1 and 2. Both already sell, both run on the same fulfillment, and white label only needs two numbers set.
