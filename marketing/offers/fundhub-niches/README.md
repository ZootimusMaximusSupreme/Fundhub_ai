## Legal notes already found

- Franchise brokers: no registration in Arizona or Florida; California registration starts **July 1, 2027** (SB 919, [UBG Law](https://www.ubglaw.com/news-and-media/california-franchise-broker-registration-law-takes-effect-july-1-2027-what-franchisors-franchise-brokers-and-franchise-sales-organizations-need-to-know)).
- SBA loans: referral fees must be disclosed on SBA Form 159.
- Mortgages: federal law (RESPA §8) bans referral fees on home loans — check this before any real estate funding offer.
