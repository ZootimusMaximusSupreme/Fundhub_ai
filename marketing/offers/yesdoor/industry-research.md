# Yesdoor industry research (2026-10-07)

Ground truth for avatars and system design. Every number is linked. "Not found" means searched and missing.

## Renters (client point of view)

- **Approvals:** 83.2% of applications were approved in 2017 (~17% denied). That is the newest hard data ([ConnectCRE](https://www.connectcre.com/stories/rental-application-approvals-are-up-where-are-highest-approval-rates/)).
- **Why denied:** accounts in default or collections caused 32.9% of rejections; criminal history ~12%; credit or income under 8% ([PA Realtors](https://www.parealtors.org/?p=29966)).
- **Scores:** approved renters averaged ~650; rejected ~538 ([Rentec](https://www.rentecdirect.com/blog/6291-2/)).
- **What landlords fear:** missed rent 84%, past evictions 56%, criminal history 45% ([SmartMove](https://smweb-uat1.transunion.com/SmartMove/blog/landlord-top-tenant-concern-study.page)).
- **Application fees:**
  - 79% of recent renters paid one; typical fee $50 ([Zillow](https://zillow.com/learn/how-much-are-apartment-application-fees)).
  - 66% sent 2+ applications ([MPA](https://www.mpamag.com/us/news/general/zillow-lists-renters-most-likely-to-pay-application-fees/212670)).
  - ~35% say upfront costs are their biggest hurdle ([HousingFinance](https://housingfinance.com/data-trends/why-renters-cant-make-their-next-move)).
- **California:**
  - Screening fee cap rises with inflation; it was $65.86 in Dec 2025 (secondhand, [CAA](https://caanet.org/kb/max-screening-fee/)).
  - AB 2493 (since Jan 2025): take the first qualified applicant, or refund the fee to those not picked ([Davis FAQ](https://www.cityofdavis.org/city-hall/social-services-and-housing/rental-resources-program/landlords-and-property-managers/faqs)).
  - No blanket criminal-record bans; arrests without a conviction can't be used; each case is reviewed one by one ([CA CRD](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/2024/06/11/civil-rights-department-submits-comment-letter-in-support-of-federal-proposal-to-reduce-barriers-to-housing-for-people-with-criminal-records/)).
- **Arizona:** no fee cap; a fee is refundable unless labeled non-refundable in writing (A.R.S. 33-1321). No statewide fair-chance housing rule found ([iPropertyManagement](https://ipropertymanagement.com/laws/tenant-background-check-arizona)).
- **Prime renter frustrations:**
  - Affordability 38%, tracking landlord messages 26%, competing with other renters 22%; 40% lost sleep during the search ([Zillow 2022](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2022-09-08-Up-all-night-40-of-renters-report-losing-sleep-when-trying-to-find-a-new-place)).
  - 43.1% saw scam listings; typical loss $400 ([Apartment List](https://apartmentlist.com/research/how-common-is-rental-fraud-scams)).
- Not found: a current national denial rate, how common criminal lookback periods are, DUI policies.

## Apartments (building point of view)

- **Fraud** (NMHC survey of 75 operators, 2023–24, [PDF](https://www.nmhc.org/globalassets/research--insight/survey/nmhc_pulse_survey_operational_impact_rental_fraud_bad_dept_full_results.pdf)):
  - 93.3% got fraudulent applications; 84.3% saw fake pay stubs or income papers; 70.7% said fraud rose.
  - Bad debt averaged $4.2M (median $800K); fraud caused 24.5% of it.
  - Fraud was behind 23.8% of eviction filings.
- Snappt: 6.4% of applications were fraudulent in 2024 ([MFE](https://www.multifamilyexecutive.com/technology/snappt-report-6-4-of-rental-applications-were-fraudulent-in-2024_o)).
- **Vacancy:** 34.4 days on average, end of 2024 ([RealPage](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/vacant-days-climbs/)).
- **Lead cost:** listing sites cost about $1,005 per lease (Yardi 2023, secondhand, [Yardi](https://www.yardi.com/news/press-releases/leasing-study-seo/)).
- **Staffing:** on-site turnover ~32.7% a year; half of operators name staffing as their top challenge ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/top-talent/property-management-historically-has-33-turnover-rate-the-pandemic-hasnt-helped-110586)).
- **Regional managers are measured on:** occupancy, NOI, bad debt, days vacant, lead-to-lease, cost per lease (vendor guides, [MRI](https://mrisoftware.com/?p=85826)).
