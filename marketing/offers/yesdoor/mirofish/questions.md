# Questions to ask MiroFish

1. Renters in Phoenix, LA and San Diego see an ad: "See every apartment that will approve you, in 60 seconds. Just your email." Who signs up, who hesitates, and why? What makes them trust the credit soft pull?
2. Leasing managers and regional VPs at large management companies hear: "Free listing, pre-approved renters, pay only when a lease is signed." What objections come up, and which ones kill the deal?
3. Owners with in-house leasing teams (REITs) get the same pitch. Do they say yes, no, or "only for hard-to-fill units"?
4. Real estate brokers and affiliates see the $29 per renter / $499 a month offer. Which plan do they pick, and what would make them switch to white-label?
5. Renters who were denied are offered a denial breakdown, then credit repair. What share buys, and at what price do they stop?
6. One year in, renters reach lease end. What share use Yesdoor again to move, and what would make more of them do it?
