# Yesdoor — seed for simulation

Yesdoor is an automated apartment placement company licensed in Arizona, California and Florida.

Renters: enter just an email, agree to a credit soft pull and background check, and see which apartment buildings will approve them in about 60 seconds. They only see buildings under contract with Yesdoor. They book a tour from the app. Placement is free to the renter. Renters who are denied or have low odds are offered a denial breakdown, credit repair, buyers guides, or a lawyer referral. At lease end, Yesdoor checks in and re-matches renters who move.

Apartments: list for free. They pay a placement fee only when a lease is signed, on their own fee terms (usually one month's rent; California 48–100%). There is a 60-day refund if the renter leaves. Listings flow from their property software (Yardi, RealPage, Entrata). Each Yesdoor renter lands in their system as a visitor record with Yesdoor as the source. Upsells: priority placement for a higher fee, and a lease-up push for new buildings. Large managers sign one portfolio agreement for all their buildings.

Brokers and affiliates: free test on 25 of their renter leads, then $29 per renter or $499 a month per office. White-label costs $10,000 plus $499 a month. Licensed partners get 25% of the placement fee on leases from their renters.

Markets: Phoenix rent ~$1,550; Los Angeles ~$2,750; San Diego ~$2,893; Miami ~$2,761; Tampa ~$2,025; Orlando ~$1,838. About 55% of US renters renew at lease end.

The biggest apartment managers: Greystar (~1.01M units), Asset Living, Willow Bridge, RPM Living, AMC, Avenue5. Owners with in-house leasing include Irvine Company, Equity Residential, AvalonBay, Essex, Camden and MAA.
