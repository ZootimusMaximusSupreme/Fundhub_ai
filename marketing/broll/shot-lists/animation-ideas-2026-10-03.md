# New b-roll ideas for the Fundhub ads

Written 2026-10-03. Read only: nothing in the repo was changed.

**B-roll** is the short animation that plays over Chris while he talks. A **template** is a ready-made animation we can use again and again with new words.

## How I counted

- I read 25 scripts. That is the 20 in `ALL-SCRIPTS-since-2026-09-02.md`, plus Ads 24, 25 and 26 (sorting hat), plus Notes Scripts 2 and 4. Earlier drafts are not counted.
- A script counts once, even if it says the line two times.
- I left out every idea the kit already has (the 17 templates), plus the flat tire, the jack and the tool match. Those three are being built now for Scripts 3 and 4. I also left out the approvals carousel, since it is being built on its own.
- **Notes Scripts 1 and 2 are the Apple Notes ads.** Today they get no b-roll, because Chris stands in front of the note. So each count also says how many scripts are left without those two.

## Rules for every clip

- One idea. 2 to 3 seconds. Clean, never busy.
- White background with the faint grid. Blue #3D86F0. Inter font. Lowercase "fundhub." wordmark.
- 1080 wide by 1920 tall. Words stay out of the top 14% and the bottom 35%.
- Every word and number on screen comes from the line the clip sits under.
- Never on screen: "credit repair", "your number", "the number", "carry", "round two", EIN, DUNS, net-30. Never show a score going up. Never promise an approval.

## Templates that would read better as a simpler version

### ProofWall: make a "proof chips only" version — 16 scripts

Today ProofWall does four things at once: a turning wall of approvals, a big amount chip, three proof chips, and the "million dollars" line. Since the approvals carousel is being built on its own, split the chips off. Show only the chips ("A decade", "Hundreds of files", "Thousands of data points") popping in, then hold. 2 seconds, one idea.

The credentials line is the most repeated line in all the scripts. It is in 16 of them: $297 Ads 1–8, $297 Shorts 1–7 (Short 7), Ad 21 ("I've seen that on hundreds of files."), Ad 23 ("I've funded a little over a million dollars for myself."), SLO VSL 1, SLO VSL 2, the Founder VSL, the 9/30 /watch VSL and the 9/30 thank-you video.

Watch the words. The older ads say "ten years" and "tens of thousands of data points". The newer ones say "a decade" and "thousands of data points". The chip words must match the line each time. The template already lets you change them. Keep the Founder VSL's "$25 million" and Koi Poke off screen: the 10-02 rules replaced that old proof.

### HiddenDataPoints: make a plain count for "one or twelve" — 5 scripts

The ring of dots is the picture for the 13 hidden data points. The shot lists also use it for other things: "12 items holding it back" (Ad 24, clip 1) and "every letter written, all six rounds" (Ad 21, clip 3). That blurs what the dots mean. A plain version reads better: one chip shows up, then eleven more snap in next to it as the count ticks from 1 to 12.

It fits "Sometimes it's one item. Sometimes it's twelve." in $297 Ads 1, 3, 4 and 5, and "Sometimes one item is holding it back, and sometimes it's twelve." in Ad 24. Keep the dot ring only for the 13.

### FileItems: make a quiet version with no flying money — used in 7 of the 34 shot-list clips

FileItems is the kit's most used template. It does very different jobs: three bureaus not matching, two paths, five business items, four risky words in a name, and "we fix both". Every red row throws a bill and every green row flips a coin. With four or five rows (Ad 23, Ad 25), that is a lot of money moving in 2.5 to 3 seconds. A quiet version would tick each row in with a small dot and keep the money faded at the sides. Long rows like Ad 24's two paths read better on Pick Your Path (idea 6).
