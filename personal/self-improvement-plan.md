# Self-improvement plan

Written 2026-10-05. Personal, not business.

The goal is to stop going backwards. The hardest losses came from a few big decisions, and the boundaries lesson came back a second time, so the plan targets repeat patterns and big decisions, plus communication and hardness.

## The six moves

1. **Improv classes.** Weekly classes are the fastest fix for being out of practice socially, and they force you to be uncomfortable in front of people. Keep speed dating and Meetup going too, with a set number of events per month.
2. **The Hoffman Process.** A 7-day residential intensive on the patterns you picked up growing up. It is built for learning the same lesson twice, like boundaries with women.
3. **A 10-day Vipassana silent retreat.** Donation-based. 10 hours of meditation a day, no phone, no talking. The hardest emotional-control training there is.
4. **A competition date.** Register for a BJJ or amateur MMA tournament 4 to 6 months out. A date on the calendar forces the training, feeds the fat loss, and builds hardness.
5. **A personal board for big decisions.** 2 or 3 people who have lost money and come back review any decision above a set dollar amount before you make it. Before you commit, write down exactly how the decision could fail.
6. **A 30-day rejection challenge.** Get told no by a stranger every day, whether that's asking for a discount or starting a conversation with a woman.

Exosomes: the FDA has approved no exosome products for any use and has warned about serious adverse events from unapproved ones. Hold off on doing a lot of them until there is better evidence.
