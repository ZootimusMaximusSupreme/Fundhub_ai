# Owner floor — every 10 showed calls (2026-10-10)

**Owner-set 2026-10-10 (Chris):** worst case, every 10 **showed** calls close:

- 1 × credit repair, $1,000
- 1 × funding client, $3,000 deposit (10% success fee later)
- 1 × $5,000 deal

This replaces the band model's mix (`ops/workflows/ads-waterfall-projections-2026-08-26.md`) as the planning floor. Chris now sets and closes his own calls.

## Why the floor holds — Chris's lead mix (owner-set 2026-10-10)

Unless leads are gated hard, 10 showed calls fall on a bell curve:

| Group | Share of 10 | Score | What they buy | Owner rate | Closes per 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top | 2 | 700+ | Funding, $3,000 retainer (not profit, a start fee) | about 1 in 2 | 1 |
| Middle | 6 | 600–700 | Financed education and systems: $5,000 (~$200/mo, 3 yrs) or $10,000 (~$400/mo, 3 yrs). Verifiable, good income. | about 1 in 2 qualified with money | up to 3 |
| Bottom | 2 | under 600 | Credit repair, $1,000 | about 1 in 2–3 | about 1 |

The floor counts 1 middle deal. The mix allows up to 3.

| Middle deals per 10 showed | Cash now per 10 (list, $5K deals) | With $10K deals |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (floor) | $9,000 | $14,000 |
| 2 | $14,000 | $24,000 |
| 3 | $19,000 | $34,000 |

Target lead: 600 or above. Under 600 is the hardest group. The pixel is being pre-seeded with buyer data so Meta finds more 600+ people.

## Booked vs showed

The tables above the lead mix were first written per booked call. Chris's floor is per showed call. At the model's 77% show rate, 10 booked calls is about 7.7 showed, so the floor is about $6,900 cash now per 10 booked ($690 per booked call). Ads still pay until a booked call costs about $690.
