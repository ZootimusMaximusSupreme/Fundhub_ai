# Video intro script

**What it is:** A short personal video. Chris records it on his business cell for each new lead and sends it by text. Then Chris follows up with a call (see `setter-call-script.md`).

**Chris's opener:** "Hey, how's it going? Nice to meet you..." That line is from the 2026-10-07 team board. The rest of the script is built around it.

**Every source:** `ops/workflows/team-setup-sarah-justice-2026-10-07/w3-setter-sources.md`

Tags: **Haynes** = Jeremy Haynes' setter SOP. **Vann** = Spencer Vann's show-rate course. **Cole** = Cole Gordon training. **Fundhub** = Chris's own documents. **adapted** = a source gave the step, the Fundhub words were written here.

## Why a video

- A video is "obviously not automated." It is personal, it stands out in their texts, and it shows you took extra effort. (Haynes)
- A text from a real person gets answered. An automated one is easy to ignore. Replies lead to confirmations, and confirmations lead to shows. (Haynes, Vann)
- Chris's own Drive plan for this, "Back End Sales System," describes the same thing: a quick selfie video with a welcome, one specific detail from their application, and what to do next. (Fundhub, adapted from Haynes)

## Rules for every video

1. **One video per lead. Say their name.** Add one real detail from what they filled out. If nothing is on file, skip that line. Never guess or invent a detail.
2. **Say who you are in the first sentence.**
3. **Keep it quick.** Haynes says "quick" and gives no number. The base script below takes about 30 seconds to say out loud. Chris sets the length.
4. **No price, no deposit, no approval, no dollar amount a lender will give, no score promise.** No "we'll get you funded." No client counts. No "$25 million." (Fundhub: never-say list in `closer-playbook-2026-08-24.md`, proof rules in `marketing/ads/RULES.md`)
5. **Proof you may use is only:** a decade in the industry, hundreds of files, thousands of data points, and a little over a million dollars funded for yourself. Use it only if it fits. (Fundhub: owner rules 2026-10-02)
6. **End with one small ask.** Reply "yes," or text a day. That is the next step, and it is easy to say yes to. (Vann, Haynes)
7. **Send it from the business cell, between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM Arizona time.** Fundhub's system holds texts at night. Do the same by hand. (Fundhub: `src/messaging/gate.mjs`)
8. **Then write it down** in the client file Notes: "Video sent, [date and time]." Justice should know a video went out. (Fundhub: `closer-handoff.md`)
9. **Film in 4K.** The owner law says every video that is not a paid ad is shot in 4K. (Fundhub: `.claude/rules/video-4k-unless-ad.md`) **Warning:** texting apps usually shrink videos before they send them. I did not test this. See open item 1 at the bottom.

## The base script

Use this for any new lead. Fill in the brackets. Then pick a variant below for the "next step" line.

> "Hey [First name], how's it going? Nice to meet you. I'm Chris, I run Fundhub.
>
> I saw [one real thing they filled out, like: you're looking for about [amount] to [use]]. I wanted to say hi myself.
>
> [Next step. Pick from the variants below.]
>
> Text me back 'yes' so I know this got to you. And text me anything you'd like us to cover. Talk soon."

**The text that goes with it** (Vann's first-contact text, adapted. Send as three short texts, one line each, like a person.)

> "Hey [First name]"
>
> "It's Chris from Fundhub"
>
> "I just sent you a quick video. I saw you put [their exact words from the application]. Can you reply 'yes' so I know it came through?"

Vann's version asks for a "yes" so the sender knows the lead saw it. (Vann)

## Variants by where the lead came from

Same base script. Only the "next step" line changes.

### Variant A. They booked a Strategy Session (apply funnel)

Next step:

> "Your Strategy Session with Justice is [day] at [time], [time zone]. Justice is one of our Senior Advisors. I'm going to tell him what you told me so he's ready for you. Be at a computer in a quiet spot, because he'll share his screen."

Source: Haynes ("I'm the guy you're talking to at [call time]"), Vann ("immediate" confirmation text), Fundhub (Josh prompt: "be at a computer in a quiet spot"). Adapted. The title "Senior Advisor" is what the Josh prompts call the closer. See open item 2.

### Variant B. They filled out the application but did not book

Next step:

> "I saw you finished the application but didn't pick a time. No pressure. If it's okay with you, I'd like to show you how this works for someone in your spot, and get you a time with Justice if it makes sense. Just text me a day that works."

Source: Cole ("free opt-in" call: reach out fast, check they got what they asked for). Haynes ("lead with value, then ask permission"). Adapted.

Haynes also suggests offering two helpful things and letting them choose one. That is called "engagement bait." **MISSING FROM SOURCES:** no two real Fundhub assets are named anywhere (such as a client win or a short FAQ video). Do not invent them. If Chris names two, add this line: "I've got two things I think will help: [asset 1] and [asset 2]. Which would you like?"

### Variant C. They bought the $297 roadmap

Next step:

> "Thanks for grabbing the roadmap. I wanted to make sure you could get into your portal and that everything looks right to you. If you'd like help with the next step, I can get you a time with Justice."

Source: Cole ("buyer" call: check they got what they bought, "customer service" frame). Fundhub: `closer-slo-pipeline-2026-09-18.md` (every roadmap buyer gets called, and they keep portal access). Adapted. If their answers show a score of 680 or better, a business, and a need this month, tell Justice right away (see `closer-handoff.md`).

### A second video, for any lead who goes quiet

Send this after a day or two of no reply, instead of another "following up" text.

> "Hey [First name], I know we're all busy. I just wanted to make sure you didn't get stuck on something. If a different time works better, text me a day and I'll move things around. And if now's not the right time, just tell me, and I'll stop bugging you."

Source: Haynes' "we're all busy" hook. His version: "Hey [Name], I know we're all busy, but I wanted to reach out because I think I can help you with [specific problem]..." Adapted. His version ends with offering a specific resource. Fundhub has none named yet. See the MISSING note in Variant B.

Do not send "Following up," "Want to jump on a quick call?" or "Haven't heard from you, are you still interested?" Haynes calls those selfish follow-ups. (Haynes)

Not used: Haynes' "red flags" text. It was written for multi-million-dollar deals and is too hard for this audience.

## After you send it

1. **Answer replies fast.** About half to two-thirds of replies are logistics: "What's the link?" "What time is it?" Answer right away. (Vann)
2. **Sales questions get tied back to the call.** If they ask "How much?" say: "Justice goes through every dollar with you on the call, before you pay anything." (Fundhub: Josh prompt. Vann: "tie down to the call.") The full list is in `setter-call-script.md`, section 7.
3. **Reward replies with a question.** If they answer, ask one more: "What would you like Justice to cover on the call?" Then pass it on. (Vann: engagement response.)
4. **Call them.** Do not wait. A video plus a call is the point. (Cole: speed to lead, and "messenger and calling is NOT either-or.")
5. **If they reply STOP, stop.** (Fundhub: `public/js/homepage-survey.js`, the survey's SMS consent.)

## Open items for Chris

1. **4K or phone default?** The owner law says film in 4K. Texting apps usually shrink videos. Chris decides: film in 4K and let the app shrink it, or film at the phone's usual setting for texts. **Default here: follow the law, film in 4K.**
2. **What to call Justice on camera.** The Josh prompts say "Senior Advisor." Variant A uses it. Chris confirms his title.
3. **Name-swap tool.** Haynes says one video can have the name swapped by an AI tool, so Chris records once. This script assumes Chris records each one himself. No tool was added.
