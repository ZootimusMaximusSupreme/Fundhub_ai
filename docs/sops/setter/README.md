These are manual SOPs for a human setter. They do not change any product journey. The journey files in `docs/journeys/` list who can reach what. They do not hold a talk order, so there is nothing here to conflict with.

## Where it all came from

Every source is listed, with a link, in `ops/workflows/team-setup-sarah-justice-2026-10-07/w3-setter-sources.md`. The tags in the files mean:

- **Cole** = Cole Gordon training in Drive.
- **Haynes** = Jeremy Haynes' setter SOP in Drive.
- **Vann** = Spencer Vann's show-rate course in Cole's ops folder.
- **Fundhub** = Chris's own documents, in Drive or in this repo.
- **adapted** = a source gave the step. The Fundhub words were written here.
- **MISSING FROM SOURCES** = no source had it. It was left blank, not guessed.

## Three things to know

1. **Prices and proof come from the repo, not the old Drive docs.** The Drive docs say the soft pull is $50, and quote "$185K in 11 days," "200 founders," and "$25 million." The repo's catalog says $32, and the owner rule of 2026-10-02 bans those numbers. None of them are in these files.
2. **The setter says no numbers.** No fee, no deposit, no approval. Justice does the money talk. This follows Chris's Josh setter rule.
3. **Older repo rules say the setter seat is AI** ("we do not hire setters," in the closer playbook and `src/ops/hire-closer.mjs`). The 2026-10-07 board says Chris sets for now. Chris's word wins, so these files were written for Chris. Those older rules were not changed.

Open choices for Chris are at the bottom of the call script and the video script.
