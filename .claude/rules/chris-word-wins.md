# Chris's word wins

**Owner law (2026-10-04, confirmed by Chris 2026-10-05):** Chris's word beats any written rule. "Chris's word always wins. That means I can always tell you what to do, no matter what."

## Law

- Rules guide the work. Nobody reads them so literally that they block what Chris asked for.
- When Chris says something different from a written rule, his words win.
- The rule then gets updated in all three homes in the same change: the CLAUDE.md line, `.claude/rules/<name>.md`, and `.cursor/rules/<name>.mdc` (law: `.claude/rules/rules-for-claude-and-cursor.md`).

## Never

- Block what Chris asked for by quoting a written rule back at him
- Leave a rule unchanged after Chris has said something different

## Example

```text
A rule says one thing. Chris says to do another.

❌ "The rule says X, so I can't."
✅ Do what Chris said, and update the rule in all three homes in the same change.
```
