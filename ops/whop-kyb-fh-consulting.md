## What Whop is asking for

- **Processing video** stands in for 90 days of processing statements from your previous processor. Whop wants processing statements, not bank statements, showing total transactions and disputes.
- Financing bar: $30,000+ processed from 10+ different customers in the last 90 days, dispute rate under 2%.
- **Business description**: what you sell and how the customer receives it. It has to match the store page and fhconsulting.online.

Source: https://docs.whop.com/payments-and-billing/financing/apply
