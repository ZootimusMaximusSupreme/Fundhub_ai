Status: **done**. Both clips render to MP4, both previews exist, typecheck is clean for my files, and I looked at the frames.

**Default props**

```ts
{
  headline: 'Sent to every lender on their list',
  inquiryLabel: 'Hard inquiries',
  declinedLabel: 'Declined',
}
```

Other headlines for other ads: Ad 22 `'Each decline makes the next one harder'`, Notes Script 4 `'A report full of hard inquiries'`.

**What it shows (registry style):** Paper copies of one file arc out to a wall of nine blank bank slabs. A red slot fills on the file for each application. Then most slabs flip over to a red "Declined". Two stay plain: no approval mark, no bank names, no amounts.

**Script lines it serves** (notes section 8):
- 9/30 /watch VSL: "...they sent your file to every lender on their list, and you came out with a couple of approvals and a credit report full of hard inquiries." This is the default. Also: "...every one of those applications left a hard inquiry behind."
- Notes Script 4: "...on the wrong file it leaves you with small approvals and a report full of inquiries."
- Ad 22: "...each decline makes the next one harder..."
- $297 Ad 2: "Same file, different bank, and every one of those applications sits on your report making the next one harder."
- $297 Ad 6: "Your application goes out, it gets shotgunned to a list of banks..."
- Founder VSL: "...apply to a few places, rack up hard inquiries..."

**On-screen words not from the notes file:** none. Each "Declined" has a small x-in-a-circle mark next to it, which is a symbol, not a word. No number is shown: the nine slots match the nine banks, so there is no made-up count.

**Two small calls I made, both from the brief:**
- The notes say a gray "Declined" chip. The brief said calm red, so it uses the kit's muted red (`TAG.bad`).
- A slot fills when each copy reaches a bank, not when it bounces back. That matches the 9/30 line: every application leaves a hard inquiry, even the ones that are not declined.
