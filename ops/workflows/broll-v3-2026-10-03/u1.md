# U1 manifest — RefundPromise · TenSeconds

Status: **done**. Both clips render to MP4. Both previews exist. Typecheck has zero errors in my files. I looked at the frames (early, middle, hero, last), the safe-zone overlay and five prop variants.

Style: Chris's 2026-10-03 update ("you can make them 3D, you just can't make them throwing money around"). So both clips use gentle 3D: the `Stage3D` camera drift, cards that come forward out of the wall, visible edge thickness, a slight tilt, layers at different depths, and soft shadows. Nothing draws money. No DollarBill, CashStack, Coin, FlatCoin, MoneyField, FlyingBill, MoneyGutters, BillFan or BackdropStage, and no bills or coins of my own. The springs are the kit's `enter` (damping 200), so nothing overshoots.

## 2. TenSeconds

| | |
|---|---|
| Composition id | `TenSeconds` |
| File | `marketing/broll/src/templates/TenSeconds.tsx` |
| Exports | `TenSeconds`, `TenSecondsProps`, `TEN_SECONDS_BASE`, `TEN_SECONDS_HERO`, `tenSecondsDefaults` |
| BASE | 90 frames (3.0 s) |
| HERO | 80 |
| Preview | `marketing/broll/previews/ten-seconds.png` (frame 80) |
| MP4 | `marketing/broll/out/u1/ten-seconds.mp4` (1080x1920, 30 fps, 90 frames, yuv420p) |
| Frame strip | `marketing/broll/out/u1/ten-seconds-frames.png` (frames 4 to 80) |

**What it shows (registry style):** A thin blue ring ticks once around a big "10" on a floating dial while the account card settles in. Each deliverable then drops into its empty slot as a row.

**Default props:**

```json
{
  "ringNumber": "10",
  "ringLabel": "About ten seconds",
  "headline": "All of it is in your account.",
  "items": ["How Much You Qualify For", "Credit Analysis Report", "Dispute Letter Pack", "Bank & Lender Match List"]
}
```

Plus optional `durationInFrames` (60 to 90) and `showSafeZones`.

- `items` takes 0 to 6 rows. With 5 or 6 rows the dial and rows get smaller so it all fits. I checked six rows: the card's shadow ends at about y 1242, inside the 1248 edge.
- `headline` is a string or null. Null with `items: []` drops the card, and the dial shows alone at a larger size.
- **Founder VSL** ("it literally takes about two minutes"): `{"ringNumber":"2","ringLabel":"About two minutes","headline":null,"items":[]}`.
- For a line that only says "Takes about ten seconds" ($297 Ads 1–5), the dial-only setting keeps every word on screen inside the spoken line.

**Timing (base frames):** dial 0 → number 4 → label chip 8 → ring sweep 6 to 40 (the ten tick marks light as it passes, then a soft blue halo) → card 10, which arrives with dashed empty slots so the first second is never half-empty → rows drop in from frame 40, one every 5 frames. Four rows are still by about frame 69.

**Script lines it serves** (from the notes file, idea 4, plus the brief):

- $297 Ads 1, 3, 5: "Takes about ten seconds." Before it: "Click below, pay the $297, fill out the form."
- $297 Ad 2: "Takes about ten seconds." and "...so you get the answer in ten seconds instead of six months."
- $297 Ad 4: "Takes about ten seconds and you'll know which card it is."
- $297 Ad 8: "Ten seconds and all of it is in your account."
- SLO VSL 1: "You fill out a form, it takes about ten seconds, and all of it is in your account."
- SLO VSL 2: "Your roadmap is in your account." and "...built off your own credit in about ten seconds."
- Portal welcome video: "Everything we build for you drops in there the second it's ready, and it's yours."
- 9/30 /watch VSL: "...the short application, which takes about ten seconds, then pick a time for your call."
- Founder VSL: "...it literally takes about two minutes." (use the props above)
- Brief: "...you get every step in order..."

**Words not from the notes file:** the four row names. They come from the /roadmap page's order summary (`marketing/landing-pages/slo/slo-01-sales.html`, lines 970–975), as the brief asked. They are not in any spoken line. If a shot list wants only words from the line, pass `items: []`. The person icon on the card has no words.

**Words conflict — W0 or Chris to call (the template does not block on it):** The page's third deliverable is named "Credit Optimization Roadmap". The batch rules ban every form of "optimize" on screen, so I left that row out of the defaults rather than rename it. I default to 4 rows, all exact page names. The template fits all six if someone passes them in `items` (checked in a still).
