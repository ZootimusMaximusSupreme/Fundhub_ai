## What he asked for, by timestamp

**Hero**
- 00:00 Sub-headline restates the headline in different words. "Some of the best pages literally have the headline, then restate what was just said."
- 00:24 Mechanizing — tell them the mechanism. Can expand.
- 00:48 $297 on the page is a direct offer. Fine if you're at or under market price. May trigger an instant "f*** that" or get filed in their research bank. **Test it, don't invent a strategy.**
- 02:03 "The guarantee is in a nice area. You can try considering it bigger" so people can see it.
- 03:12 "credit file" → business owners think in **credit score**. Make it clearer to them.
- 03:36 "and we'll get you there" — what do you mean by *there*? Clarify the end state (funded). **He calls this low priority; it already works.**

**Pain**
- 02:26 You're assuming what the pain is. Sometimes better not to say it and let them imagine it.
- 04:47–05:19 He reads the whole block: "that's true… you really painted the picture well. It's actually really good writing." → **Keep it. Test before heavy edits.**
- 05:41 Show, don't tell. "Watching how your inquiries pile up… therefore / which means…"
- 06:07 Needs a connector or bridge into the last line: "**And what they haven't told you is that** the condition of your credit determines where you land."

**Bridge / proof claims**
- 06:32 "We spent thousands of hours building the system that reads your file the way a lender does" — good.
- 06:57 "the way a lender's **underwriter** reads it" — more clarity, optional.
- 07:23 Connector word before "and you get all of this."
- 08:10 "Ten years, hundreds of files, **tens of thousands** of data points" — too big, tips into unbelievable. **Thousands is dope.** Natural progression: ten → hundreds → thousands.
- 11:42 I vs we — depends whether the voice is Fundhub or Chris. He introduces himself, so either is fine.
- 12:08 "Nobody else in this industry **sells** this" → "**offers this type of help**." Help opens people up; selling closes them.
- 12:53 "there's no shortcut to that" can be **indirect** — let ten years of reading files imply it.

**What you get**
- 09:21 "Here's What Comes With It" restates the kicker. Delete it → "**Here's what you get**." Make it about **them**.
- 09:43 Deliverables don't have to be fancy. Clear and direct. No cleverness.

**Offer**
- 10:30 "Why $297, most people pay…" — **he likes it. Keep.**
- 10:30 Guarantee could be a little stronger (offer building, his words — vague on purpose).
- 10:53 End state: why are they buying? **To get funded.** "$297 gives you the exact method to unlock it. You unlock funding" — not only "the finished file."

**Who it's for / CTAs**
- 13:40 "the most funding your file can possibly get" — optional expand ("highest limit funding possible").
- 14:05 "Whether your credit is clean right now" — people don't know what clean means. Reframe: "**Every day you wait, you leave money on the table, not knowing how much your file can get you.**"
- 14:55 "This is for you if you want to know exactly" — good disqualifier, keep.
- 15:19 Benefits then removed pain. Keep it simple.
- 16:07 Step 3 "see exactly how much you qualify for" → "**how much your file is worth**." Double down on the claim.
- 16:07 "**what** you qualify for" is vague — a loan? a card? Use **how much**.
- 16:30 Close headline: "Find out what your file is worth" — try a **question headline**: "How much is your file worth? Find out today in less than 10 seconds."
- 16:30 FAQ "Our best clients had these same questions" — **good.**

**Checkout**
- 16:54 Order summary: **restate the guarantee for sure.** Preface again what they get. Restate the guarantee a couple of times.
- 17:18 "So what happens when you press the button" is repetitive with the top. **You could lose them.** Every word must earn its spot. → **DELETE**
- 18:04 "Why we ask for your social" — opens you up to an unnecessary objection. Serious people don't bat an eye. **"I don't think you should add this here."** → **DELETE**
- 19:18 "First box is your name, email, phone. Second box is the short form" — "I wouldn't do that." Earning words. → **DELETE**
- 19:42 Card logos / payment reassurance: "most people know… this is a little old… I don't think it's very much necessary." → **DELETE**
- 20:04 Bottom: "I would [move the] guarantee up, and then Why $297 — I just don't think it's needed anymore after you said it once. This honestly could be deleted completely. **And then you just end it with testimonials here.**" → **DELETE both bottom cards**
- 20:29 "Same thing with this scrolling stuff" (the marquee). Every word must earn its place. → **DELETE**
- 20:29 Closing frame: "these are all just opinions. The only way to truly know is to delete and test that s***."
