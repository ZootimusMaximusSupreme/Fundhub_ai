## The short answer

| # | Finding |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Nobody owns 55+ apartment placement in Phoenix.** No Phoenix apartment locator markets 55+. No locator or placement agency shows up in the top 20 search results for "55+ apartments phoenix" (Brave Search, Oct 9, 2026). |
| 2 | **Two operators own most of the units.** Phoenix has about 16 modern 55+ rental communities with about 2,800 units **(our math)**. Sparrow (Mera, Amberlin, Sage) has about 1,560 and Greystar (Album, Overture) about 846. Two deals would cover about 86% of the units **(our math; tying Amberlin and Sage to Sparrow is our inference)**. |
| 3 | **They need renters now.** Phoenix 55+ is 88.7% full, the lowest of the 15 biggest markets, which is about 300 empty units **(our math)**. They're giving away 1–2.5 months of free rent. |
| 4 | **The big senior agencies are weak in 55+.** Only 1% of active-adult renters used a paid referral service. 28% found their community online. A Place for Mom lists Phoenix 55+ apartments, but 55+ is a side line for it. |
| 5 | **The real rivals are the apartment sites.** Zillow charges when the lease is signed and sells one application that goes to many properties. Apartment List pays renters cash back on every listing. Neither shows a 55+ renter who will approve them before the tour. |
| 6 | **Nobody publishes a Phoenix 55+ locator fee, and every deal found pays at or after move-in.** Getting paid at signing with net-30 is a new ask. Zillow is the closest precedent. |
| 7 | **5:1 needs cheap leads.** At a $1,750 fee and a 5% lease rate, 5:1 needs leads at $17.50 or less **(our math)**. General real estate Meta leads average $13.74. Senior-specific leads run $40–$120. |

## What it means for Yesdoor (our inference)

- **The opening:** nobody shows a 55+ renter which communities will approve them before the tour, and nobody owns 55+ placement in Phoenix.
- **The pitch to communities:** a fee owed at lease signing that is clearly less than the 1–2 months of free rent they already give away. Operators already pay $1,000–$2,500 per move-in to residents and realtors.
- **First deals:** Sparrow and Greystar, which together hold about 86% of the units.
- **Risks:**
  - The pool is small: about 2,800 units and about 300 empty.
  - 55+ renters decide slowly, often after selling a house.
  - Many are stretched on rent.
  - Communities have never paid at signing.
  - 5:1 only works if leads cost about $17.50 or less and about 5% lease.
- **Side lane:** buyers for for-sale 55+ communities pay at closing (Robson 3%, or about a 25% agent referral, about $3,080 on a $493k home **(our math)**).
