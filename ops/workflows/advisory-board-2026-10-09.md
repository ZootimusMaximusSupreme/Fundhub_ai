## How advisors usually work

| Item | Norm | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pay | Stock, not cash. Founder Institute's free FAST template: 0.50% at the earliest stage for monthly meetings, 1.00% if they also make introductions. | [fi.co/fast](https://fi.co/fast) |
| Vesting | Over 2 years, with a short cliff (3–6 months) | FAST · [First Citizens/SVB](https://innovation.firstcitizens.com/startup-insights/startup-strategy/building-your-startup-advisory-board) |
| Time | A few hours a month, about 12–15 hours a quarter | Same |
| Front door | Former officials often work through a firm: Hogan Lovells (Sinema), BridgeCounsel (Alvarez), Avenida (Guzman). Big names "collect advisory chairs" and are hard to reach. | Lane B sources |

**Our math:** 10 advisors at 0.5–1% each is 5–10% of the company. Most startups start with 3–5.
