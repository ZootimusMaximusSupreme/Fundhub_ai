# Fundhub: disclosed card-stacking marketplace, research (2026-10-09)

Chris's idea (not decided): a marketplace of up to 10 lenders. Each borrower is verified (face scan, ID, Plaid income) and approved for several cards or loans at once, and every lender sees the full stack.

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| The FTC sued a "business credit" firm (Nov 2024) that opened many cards in owners' names. A bank sent it a cease-and-desist calling it a "credit card stacking scheme." | https://www.ftc.gov/node/77778 |
| Seed Consulting (FTC, 2021): $3–4k fees for card stacking, income inflated. $2.1M judgment and a permanent ban. | https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2021/01/ftc-acts-stop-nevada-companies-charging-consumers-thousands-open-credit-cards-pay-training-schemes |
| Issuer speed limits (unofficial, from points blogs): Chase 5/24, and Citi 1 card per 8 days, 2 per 65 days | https://militarymoneymanual.com/credit-card-application-rules/ |
| TransUnion (2017): stacked or fraud-trait personal loans went 90+ days late 11.02% of the time, against 6.22% for all loans | https://newsroom.transunion.com/transunion-bolsters-fraud-prevention-exchange-as-online-fraudsters--continue-to-impact-personal-loan-delinquency-rates/ |
| Merchant cash advance contracts commonly ban stacking | https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/stacking-prohibited |
| Bank of America is on Nav (a lender can't pay for better placement). Nav has about 70 lenders and 90 card issuers. | https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=93790 |
| Lendio has 75+ partners. Banks send the applicants they decline to Lendio. | https://finovate.com/lendio-launches-lender-turndown-program/ |
| Union Credit shows firm credit union pre-approvals: 50+ credit unions, $106M+ in loans in its first year (company figures) | https://www.unioncredit.app/2023/07/union-credit-launches-a-marketplace-for-credit-unions/ |
| Face scans on payments: Allied Irish Banks (up to €10k), banks in Thailand and Kakao Bank in Korea, a JPMorgan/PopID pilot. US banks rarely do it beyond the phone's Face ID. | https://idtechwire.com/major-irish-bank-turns-to-selfie-biometrics-for-high-value-mobile-transactions |

**Bottom line (our inference):**
- Lenders dislike stacking itself, not marketplaces.
- Verification plus disclosure fixes the "hidden" problem but not the extra risk of several accounts at once.
- Big card issuers would likely refuse. Credit unions and smaller lenders that already buy pre-approved demand are the realistic first partners.
- Fundhub must never look like the stacking schemes the FTC has sued.
