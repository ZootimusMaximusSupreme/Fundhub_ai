FAQ

Short answers.

Do you do the operations work for me?

No. You write the procedures, keep the records, and run the week. We teach the process and give you the templates. On Operations Intensive, review sessions are instruction on work you already did.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We make no promises about revenue, profit, hiring, or any business outcome. What you get is the curriculum and the templates for the program you buy.

Can I pay over time?

If a payment plan appears at checkout, it is offered by a third-party payment provider, not by FH Consulting. That provider decides approval under its own terms.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days of purchase if you have used less than 20% of the program content. Details are in the Refund Policy.
